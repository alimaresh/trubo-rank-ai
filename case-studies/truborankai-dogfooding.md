# How TruboRankAI Uses TruboRankAI

This founder workflow documents how TruboRankAI uses its own product to review the public website, prioritize improvements, and keep SEO, AEO, GEO, and AI visibility decisions tied to evidence.

**Official live study:** [TruboRankAI Dogfooding Case Study](https://truborankai.com/case-study-truborankai-dogfooding)

## The Six-Step Operating Loop

1. **Scan the website.** Review technical SEO, AI discoverability, AEO structure, GEO clarity, AI-readable resources, and page-level issues.
2. **Review search data.** Use Google Search Console context to understand queries, impressions, clicks, page movement, and content gaps.
3. **Track crawler discovery.** Monitor AI crawlers and search bots to see which public pages are being requested.
4. **Prioritize content opportunities.** Decide which pages should be improved or created based on evidence and user intent.
5. **Improve public documentation.** Clarify product pages, tool descriptions, case studies, integrations, and machine-readable resources.
6. **Monitor and refine.** Publish reviewed snapshots, compare signals over time, and choose the next action.

## What This Workflow Avoids

Dogfooding provides a practical guardrail against:

- publishing pages without a clear visitor or search purpose;
- treating crawler visits as guaranteed rankings or citations;
- making technical changes without checking discoverability;
- presenting early signals as completed outcomes;
- building product features without testing them on a real operating website.

## What Is Measured

The workflow can combine different evidence layers without confusing them:

- deterministic website scan findings;
- Google Search Console context;
- AI and search crawler activity;
- attributed AI-platform referral traffic;
- saved answer-engine observations;
- documented implementation actions.

Each evidence type answers a different question. A crawler request shows access. A referral shows an attributed visit. A saved answer shows an observed appearance. None automatically proves why an AI platform selected, ranked, cited, or recommended a page.

## Why This Is a Live Case Study

The workflow remains active as the product and website evolve. It is not presented as a finished success story. The value is the repeatable decision process: inspect evidence, prioritize a bounded improvement, document the change, and review the next reliable snapshot.

This makes the study useful to SaaS founders, developers, marketers, and SEO teams looking for an evidence-based way to connect technical SEO, Answer Engine Optimization, Generative Engine Optimization, content clarity, and crawler analytics.
